# Allowances — ticket index

- `01-list-tabs-filters-and-totals/` — Summary tiles, tabs All / Owner prescribed / Miscellaneous, search, status and trade filters, and the allowance list grouped as in the original (owner prescribed first, then miscellaneous by trade).
- `02-allowance-lines-and-budget-apply/` — An owner prescribed allowance adds a trade-item line to every trade (and to trades added later); a miscellaneous one to its trade. Bidders price the line on Scope match and it counts in their bid. The panel can push one budget amount to every bidder of a trade.
