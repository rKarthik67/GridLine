# Scope match (`pageKey=scope-match`) — ticket index

- `01-scope-item-answer-grid/` — The per-bid scope grid: typing/pasting Included/Excluded/Price/amount/formula/text into cells, optimistic recalculation, colours/notes, coverage bars, section collapse/expand, the base-bid/adjustments/total rows, and the presence "who's here" row deep link.
- `02-scope-item-row-management/` — Inserting, deleting, reordering, indenting and heading-toggling trade-item rows, plus bulk-applying a scope kit template.
- `03-zone-breakdown-entries/` — The locked Zones section where each bidder's per-zone amount is entered (or, for the remainder zone, automatically calculated as bid total minus the other zones).
- `04-bid-lane-management-and-award-selection/` — Adding a bidder as a new lane, reordering/hiding lanes, and picking (or clearing) the carried/awarded bid for the trade.
- `05-export-scope-match/` — Exporting the current trade's scope comparison as an Excel workbook or a blank PDF trade package for subcontractors.
- `06-internal-estimate-tab/` — The trade's own quantity × unit-cost estimate worksheet, kept separate from the bid totals.
- `07-scratch-pad-tab/` — A free-form 8×24 per-trade scratch grid with simple `=` arithmetic, saved per cell on blur.
- `08-trade-files-tab/` — Uploading, listing, downloading and deleting a trade's documents (drawings, proposals, bid forms), optionally tagged to a bidder.
- `09-qualifications-tab/` — Managing per-trade or project-wide assumptions/exclusions/qualifications, including client-visibility flags and reusable qualification sets.
- `10-bid-inbox-online-invites-and-submissions/` — Reviewing bid-portal invite status, resending/withdrawing links, and accepting or rejecting a subcontractor's online submission into the bid lane.
- `11-bid-inbox-proposal-reader/` — Uploading a subcontractor's proposal PDF, reviewing the background reader's suggested base amount/line answers with quoted evidence, and applying chosen suggestions to the bid lane.
- `12-trade-alternates-and-unit-prices-tabs/` — Trade tabs for pricing alternates & VE and unit prices; allowance lines tagged and locked in the Scope items grid.
