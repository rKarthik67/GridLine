## Title
Scope match — Bid lane management and award selection

### Summary
Lets the estimator add a new trade partner's bid as a lane on the grid, reorder or hide/show lanes (bid columns), and pick (or clear the pick of) the bid that's carried as the trade's awarded price. These actions operate on the `Bid`/lane columns that frame the scope grid from tickets 01–03.

### User story
As an estimator, I want to add bidders as columns, control which columns are visible and in what order, and pick the winning bid, so that the grid shows exactly the lanes I'm comparing and the rest of the app (Bid board, Price roll-up) carries the number I picked.

### Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The "+ Bidder" button (far-right header cell, `canEdit` only) opens `AddBidModal` (shared with the Bid board): typing a company name live-searches existing trade partners (`GET /partners?search=`); picking a match pre-fills email; typing a new name creates a new `TradePartner` (flagged "added by user", shown with a `*` superscript in the lane header); optional email and optional starting base bid.
- [ ] Adding a bidder that already has a non-archived `Bid` on this trade (same `PartnerId`) is rejected with a 409 "duplicate" error ("{name} is already bidding on this trade.").
- [ ] A new lane gets the next `LaneNo` (`max(LaneNo)+1` among lanes `> 0`); if a starting base amount is given the bid's `State` becomes `posted`, otherwise `invited`.
- [ ] The "Columns" menu (fx-bar) lists every bid (including hidden ones) with a checkbox-style label (`☑`/`☐`, lane letter, bidder name) toggling `hiddenInScope`; its own label shows "Columns · N hidden" when any are hidden; "View all columns" clears every hide flag at once. Hidden bids are excluded from the rendered grid, the coverage/lowest-total math, and the "N/M posted" counts, but remain selectable to re-show.
- [ ] Each visible lane header (when `canEdit`) has move-left/move-right buttons that swap its `LaneNo` with its immediate visible neighbour, and an "x" button that hides it (adds it to `hiddenInScope` without archiving the `Bid`).
- [ ] The Pick control (per lane, bottom of the header cell) toggles `IsCarried`: clicking an unpicked, posted bid picks it (toast "Picked {bidderName}"); clicking the currently-picked bid clears the pick (toast "Pick cleared"); clicking an unposted bid is rejected client-side with a toast "{bidderName} hasn't posted a price yet" (no request sent).
- [ ] A disqualified or declined bid can never be picked (`ApiException.Bad`, "A disqualified or declined bid can't be picked.").
- [ ] At most one bid per trade can be `IsCarried` at a time — picking a new one clears the previous pick in the same request.
- [ ] The trade bar shows "Picked · {name}" (bold) or "Nothing picked" (in red) reflecting the current carried bid.
- [ ] Every lane/pick mutation (`lanes`, `pick`, add bidder) updates this tab's local `trade` state, pushes the same `Trade` object into the Bid board's cached list (`syncBoard`), and — for pick — invalidates the Price roll-up query, since the carried value feeds both.
- [ ] All of the above is unavailable (buttons/menus hidden, Pick disabled) when `!canEdit` (viewer access or a locked version).

### Technical spec
**Frontend:** `web/src/pages/project/ScopeMatch.tsx` (`pick()`, `lanes()`, `moveLane()`, the lane-header JSX, the "Columns" `Menu`, the `addBid` modal state) plus the shared `AddBidModal` from `web/src/pages/project/BidBoard.tsx`. Calls: `api.put<TradeReply>('/trades/{tradeId}/pick', { bidId })` (`bidId: null` to clear); `api.put<Trade>('/trades/{tradeId}/lanes', { order, hidden })` (`order` is the full array of visible+hidden bid ids in the new `LaneNo` sequence, or `null` to leave order unchanged; `hidden` is the full list of bid ids that should be hidden, or `null` to leave hidden flags unchanged); `api.post<TradeReply>('/trades/{tradeId}/bids', { bidderName, partnerId, email, baseAmount })` from `AddBidModal`. Response shapes: `Trade`/`TradeReply` from `web/src/api/types.ts` (`Trade.bids: Bid[]`, each with `rank`, `isPosted`, `isCarried`, `totalAmount`, `hiddenInScope`).
**Backend:** All in `Endpoints/TradeEndpoints.cs`. `PUT /trades/{id:long}/pick` — requires capability `trade.pick`, `Access.Trade` (editor+); validates the target bid belongs to the trade and isn't `disqualified`/`declined`; clears any existing carried bid then sets the new one; recalculates (`Recalc.TradeAndSummary`, reason `trade.pick`); logs verb `bid.pick` or `bid.unpick`. `POST /trades/{id:long}/bids` — `Access.Trade` (editor+); delegates to `TradeEndpoints.AddBid` (resolves/creates `TradePartner` by name, resolves/creates `PartnerPerson` for email/contact, rejects a duplicate partner on the trade with 409, assigns the next `LaneNo`, initial `State`/`IsPosted` from whether a base amount was given); recalculates; logs `bid.create`. `PUT /trades/{id:long}/lanes` — `Access.Trade` (editor+); validates `Order` (if given) lists every current bid on the trade exactly once (400 otherwise); renumbers `LaneNo` via a temporary `+10000` offset to avoid unique-constraint collisions mid-update; sets `HiddenInScope = (BidId = ANY(@hidden))` for every bid on the trade when `Hidden` is given; logs `trade.lanes`. Related: `PATCH /bids/{id}` and `DELETE /bids/{id}` (ticket 01 covers base-bid editing; delete is not exposed on this tab's UI but exists on the Bid board).
**Database:** `Bid` (`BidId` PK, `TradeId` FK→`Trade` cascade, `ProjectId` FK→`Project` cascade — auto-filled by trigger `TrgBidProject`/`SetProjectId()`, `PartnerId`/`PersonId`, `BidderName`, `LaneNo smallint`, `State` enum `BidState`, `IsPosted`, `IsCarried`, `BaseAmount`, `BaseSource`, `ScopeAdjustments DEFAULT 0`, `TotalAmount`, `HiddenInScope boolean DEFAULT false` (added in `005_scope_parity.sql`), `ArchivedAt`, `RowRev DEFAULT 1`; `UNIQUE (TradeId, LaneNo)`). Unique partial index `UxOneCarriedBid ON Bid (TradeId) WHERE IsCarried AND ArchivedAt IS NULL` — this is the actual DB-level guarantee that only one bid per trade can be carried (not just application logic). Index `IxBidProject (ProjectId)`. Trigger `TrgTouch` on `Bid` bumps `RowRev`/`ChangedAt`. `TradePartner`/`PartnerPerson` are created on demand by `AddBid` (directory tables outside this page's primary scope).

### Dependencies
- Shares `Trade`/`Bid` state with ticket `01-scope-item-answer-grid.md` (lane order/visibility and the picked bid directly change what that grid renders) and with the Bid board page (`pageKey=bid-board`), which is the primary place bids/trades are created and where `AddBidModal`/`AddTradeModal` also live.
- The picked bid's `TotalAmount`/`CarriedValue` feed the Price roll-up page — picking/unpicking invalidates `['price-roll-up', vid]`.
- See `_Overview/Architecture.md`: Authorization/scoping (`Access.Trade`), Unit of work, Recalculation engine (`Recalc.TradeAndSummary`).

### Notes / edge cases
- `UxOneCarriedBid` is a **partial unique index**, not a check constraint — a race between two tabs simultaneously picking different bids on the same trade will surface as a Postgres `23505` (mapped by `ErrorHandling.Map` to a generic 409 "duplicate" message), not the friendly "pick cleared first" flow the single-request handler assumes.
- `PUT /trades/{id}/lanes` with `Order` renumbers via a `+10000` offset first specifically to dodge the `UNIQUE (TradeId, LaneNo)` constraint during the swap — a bulk reorder is therefore two `UPDATE` passes inside one transaction, not one.
- Hiding a lane (`hiddenInScope`) does not remove its `ScopeAnswer` rows or its contribution to the trade's calculated totals — it only hides the column in the Scope match UI; the hidden bid's price still counts toward lowest-value/rank comparisons unless also excluded elsewhere.
- Deleting a bid (`DELETE /bids/{id}`, available from the Bid board, not from this tab) reassigns it a negative `LaneNo` (`-1 - (BidId % 30000)`) rather than truly freeing the lane number — a factor if any code assumes `LaneNo` is always positive for archived bids.
- `AddBidModal` is defined in `BidBoard.tsx` and imported into `ScopeMatch.tsx` — a change to its fields affects both pages identically; there is no Scope-match-specific variant.
