# Unit prices — ticket index

- `01-list-and-add/` — Lists unit prices with unit, selected price, quoted price and status; adds new ones from a modal.
- `02-select-price-and-quoted-price/` — The expanded row edits the unit price and shows every trade's price with a Select button, then Selected price, Discounted price, each indirect with its share of the contract and an on/off switch, and the Quoted price.
