# Alternates & VE — ticket index

- `01-list-filter-and-totals/` — Lists every alternate (ALT-n) and value-engineering idea (VE-n) on the version with its total quoted, status and last change, plus KPI tiles for each kind.
- `02-edit-panel-and-calculation/` — The expanded row edits description, quoted %, status and comments, and shows the trade amounts, trade total, subtotal quoted, each indirect with an on/off switch, and the total quoted.
- `03-add-delete-and-bulk-status/` — Add an alternate or VE idea from a modal; select rows to set a status or delete them in one go.
